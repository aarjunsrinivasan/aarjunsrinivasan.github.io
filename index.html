About Writing Projects Experience Publications

I'm a research engineer who enjoys learning new domains and figuring out where AI can help, across manufacturing, materials science, and healthcare. In all of them my focus is making AI systems more robust: testing them, evaluating where they fail, and improving them until the results are reliable.

For six years at Fraunhofer USA I've taken applied ML from research to production. My work has spanned training generative models (diffusion, controlled editing, unsupervised latent edits), building AI-assisted human annotation workflows, and robustness evaluation tool for the US Navy. Now I focus on post-training vision-language models. Outside of work I run controlled experiments on how models are built and trained: architecture trade-offs, training correctness, throughput and memory efficiency, and the math underneath. The writing and projects below are where that work lives.
